Privacy policy

Ringscope Publisher · effective 11 November 2026

What this covers

Ringscope Publisher is a small program the Ringscope channel uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel. It runs only on the channel owner's computer. It is not a public service: no one else signs in to it, and it has no website, server or database of its own.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload Ringscope's videos and set their thumbnails.
  • See, edit, and permanently delete your YouTube videos, ratings, comments and captions (youtube.force-ssl): to upload caption files, add videos to the channel's playlists, and read back the channel name and each upload's processing status. The tool never deletes videos, ratings or comments.

It uses YouTube API Services only for the Ringscope channel, and reads nothing from other channels or users.

What it stores

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's computer, in a private folder that is never shared or published. Upload records (video IDs and publish times) are kept in the channel's own project files. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google's YouTube API.

Sharing

No data from Google accounts is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Ringscope Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

Ringscope Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (choose "Ringscope Publisher", then remove access). The locally stored token then stops working.

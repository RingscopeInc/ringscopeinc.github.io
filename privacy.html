Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

In short
  • This website has no accounts, no forms, no ads, no cookies and no tracking.
  • On YouTube and TikTok, we only see the same anonymous statistics every channel sees.
  • If you email us, we use your address only to reply.
  • We never sell or share personal information.

This website

ringscopeinc.github.io is a simple information page. It doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and it doesn't ask you for any information.

Two outside services are involved in showing it to you:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google to download them. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Our videos on YouTube and TikTok

When you watch, like, comment on or follow Ringscope on YouTube or TikTok, those platforms handle your data under their own policies: Google/YouTube and TikTok.

As the channel, we see what every creator sees: totals such as views, watch time, likes, followers and broad audience statistics (for example, countries or age ranges). These numbers are anonymous. We use them to decide which videos to make next. Comments you post are public on those platforms; we may reply to them or answer a question from one in a future video, without naming you unless your comment is already public under your name.

If you email us

If you write to ringscopeinc@gmail.com, we use your email address and message only to reply. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your message. Ask us and we'll delete our copy.

How we publish our videos

Ringscope uploads its own videos with its own tools, which run on the Ringscope studio's computer. These tools connect only to Ringscope's own accounts: they use the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services) to upload and manage Ringscope's videos, and the YouTube Analytics API to read Ringscope's own video statistics. They don't access any viewer's account or anyone else's channel, and the sign-in details stay on that computer. Ringscope's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Children

Ringscope is made for a general audience of teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
